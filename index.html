The Existing System

A Workforce Trapped in Yesterday

Rural employment in India still runs on word-of-mouth, middlemen, and missed opportunities. The result: wasted days, unfair wages, and a women's workforce locked out of livelihood.

Waste of Time

Employers spend days locating labourers. Workers waste hours seeking jobs that may never come.

No Direct Communication

There is no reliable channel between rural employers and the workers who need the income.

Middlemen Dependence

Agents extract a cut from every job. Workers lose wages. Employers overpay. Trust erodes.

Lack of Information

No structured data on a worker's skill, availability, or location. Decisions are made on rumours.

Unfair Wages

Workers are sometimes paid less than promised or not paid at all — with no record to dispute it.

Women Locked Out

Women labourers receive fewer offers because traditional networks are dominated by men.

No Digital Adoption

Urban India lives on apps. Rural India is still offline for its most important economic activity.

Contact Difficulty

Phone numbers and skill profiles of workers are simply not accessible to those who need them.

Every day without this app, thousands of workers miss a fair wage — and thousands of jobs go unfilled.

Investor FAQ

Common Questions, Clear Answers

Everything an investor, partner, or policymaker needs to evaluate VLF in a single read.

One rupee equals one Gold Coin. Employers buy coins (₹100 → 100 coins, ₹500 → 500 coins) and use them to pay workers directly on the app. Workers cash out anytime. Every transaction is logged, secure, and transparent — closing the loop on wage default and cash mishandling.
Every user logs in via their Aadhar number authenticated with a one-time password. This eliminates fake accounts, confirms genuine identity, and creates a trustworthy environment for both workers and employers from day one.
Employers find available labourers within 30 km of their location — wide enough to ensure supply, tight enough to keep commute and cost rational for daily-wage work. The result: instant matches, lower travel cost, less idle time.
The platform tracks completed jobs per worker per year. At 50 jobs, the worker receives bonus Gold Coins. At 100 jobs, they unlock insurance protection covering work-site accidents and injuries — turning loyalty into security.
Traditional hiring is dominated by male word-of-mouth networks. By making profiles location- and skill-searchable, VLF brings women workers directly into the employer's view — providing equal opportunity, fair wages, and independent income on the same platform as men.
Yes. Phase 1 covers every district of Karnataka. Phase 2 expands across Indian states. Future phases add Hindi and English, AI-driven recommendations, voice search, government scheme integration, and broader insurance and health benefits — built on the same Firebase cloud foundation.
